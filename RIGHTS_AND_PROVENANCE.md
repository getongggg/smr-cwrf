# Rights and provenance

This is a curated research archive, not a blanket redistribution of all project assets.
No new permissive license is asserted for the original research code in this release.
Rights remain with the respective rights holders; an open-source license can be added
after authorship and institutional permissions are confirmed. Public visibility alone
does not grant a general license to reuse the code.

Third-party datasets, simulator assets, pretrained weights and vendored implementations
are not included. Obtain them from their original providers under their own terms.
Internal reviews, private correspondence, company material, personal profile details,
account credentials and the complete manuscript are excluded.

`EXPORT_MANIFEST.json` records workspace-relative origins and SHA-256 checksums.
Historical metadata may contain original project paths or historical file hashes;
they are provenance, not a promise that every original artifact is redistributed.
Original research files remain unchanged by this export.
